WHAT PULSEFIT DOES

Five ways it changes

Every feature is engineered around one goal — helping you understand and improve your health without thinking about it.

Medical-grade sensors continuously track heart rate, SpO₂, and stress levels — delivering biometric insights you can actually act on, 24 hours a day.

Smart Sleep Tracking

Advanced stage analysis monitors deep, REM, and light cycles to optimize your recovery window and help you wake up genuinely refreshed.

5ATM water resistance and dedicated swim tracking mean PulseFit never needs to come off — through rain, surf, and the open sea.
